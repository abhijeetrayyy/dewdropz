Twenty-four screens covering the whole app: first launch, sign-in, browse, search, buy, pay, track, and the account. One icon set, one type scale, one navigation bar, one set of spacing rules — applied without exception, so the app reads as a single product rather than a folder of ideas.
WHAT CHANGED FROM v2
interests Real icon set. Material Symbols Rounded, 24px grid, weight 400, FILL 1 for active states. No typographic glyphs anywhere. format_size Five type sizes, no more. 34 / 26 / 21 display, 17 title, 15 body, 13 meta, 11 label. Every screen draws from that list only. bottom_navigation Platform tab bar. Full-width, 83pt with safe area, pill indicator on the active tab. Identical on every tabbed screen. grid_4x4 8pt spacing, four radii. Gaps are 8/12/16/20/24. Radii are 12 chip, 20 card, 28 sheet, 999 pill. Nothing in between.

PACKING LISTS · 7 MIN
The Kedarkantha packing list
SG
Swastik Ghosh Dastidar
Guide · 41 summits · Dehradun
December on Kedarkantha is not cold until it is. The summit push starts at 4am and the wind finds every gap you left open.
Most people over-pack the base layers and under-pack the shell. You will sweat before sunrise on the climb out of Sankri, then stand still for twenty minutes at the ridge while somebody's crampon gets fixed. That twenty minutes is what your kit is actually for.
